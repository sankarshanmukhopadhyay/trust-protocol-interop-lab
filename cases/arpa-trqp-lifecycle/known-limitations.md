# Known limitations — IC-ARPA-TRQP-HIST-001

This case is a **Candidate semantic-composition experiment**, not an interoperability-tested implementation claim.

## Not established at Candidate maturity

- The read-only TRQP projection is not authoritative ARPA state and cannot create, restore, extend or revoke ARPA authority.
- Historical validity does not imply present validity or present consequence. Requested-time state and current state must remain distinguishable.
- No executed evidence manifest currently binds the ARPA/TRQP composition to reproducible run results.
- The case does not establish production TRQP service behaviour, wire-protocol conformance, external certification, or upstream endorsement.
- Results remain scoped to the pinned ARPA and TRQP baselines; material baseline drift requires reassessment.
- Projection availability, archival completeness and operational retention guarantees are outside this candidate unless explicitly represented by evidence.

## Promotion condition

Promotion to `interoperability-tested` requires a deterministic evaluator, reproducible positive and negative execution results, and an evidence manifest binding vectors, invariants, ownership, limitations, baselines and the bounded historical-resolution claim.
