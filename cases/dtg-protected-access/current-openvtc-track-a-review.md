# Review checklist

- The target is the exact current OpenVTC revision selected by RAHP #439, not Dogwood.
- Dogwood code and evidence remain historical and unchanged.
- The positive control and pressure case use the same current-target adapter.
- The pressure case does not seed a shared client identifier.
- `executed_surfaces` prevents non-execution from being classified as absence.
- The workflow verifies the upstream checkout is clean after execution.
- DPIP export is produced from the actual runtime capture.
- No favourable observation is promoted to a privacy or assurance judgment in the producer.
