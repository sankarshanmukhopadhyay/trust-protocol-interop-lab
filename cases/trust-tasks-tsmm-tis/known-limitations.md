# Known limitations — IC-TT-TSMM-TIS-001

This case is a **Candidate semantic-composition experiment**, not an interoperability-tested implementation claim.

## Not established at Candidate maturity

- No executed evidence manifest currently binds the Trust Tasks, TSMM and TIS composition to reproducible run results.
- The case does not establish wire-level interoperability, production deployment behaviour, external certification, or conformance of any referenced project.
- The mapping tests separation of task state, trust decision, evidence and effect admission; it does not assert that TSMM or TIS acquires authority over Trust Tasks semantics.
- The pinned Trust Tasks, TSMM and TIS baselines are the scope of this candidate. Baseline drift requires reassessment before results can be carried forward.
- Runtime authority re-evaluation, duplicate-execution protection, partial-application disposition and other consequential events remain meaningful only when their distinct ownership and evidence lineage are preserved.

## Promotion condition

Promotion to `interoperability-tested` requires a deterministic evaluator, reproducible execution results and an evidence manifest that binds the exercised vectors, invariants, ownership, limitations, baselines and bounded claim scope.
