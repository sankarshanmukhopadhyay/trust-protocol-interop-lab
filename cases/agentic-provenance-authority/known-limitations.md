# Known limitations — IC-AGENT-PROVENANCE-AUTH-001

This Candidate status is bounded by the following limitations.

1. **No executed cross-implementation evidence yet.** The vectors encode expected behavior; they do not demonstrate live interoperability.
2. **CAWG-TRQP agentic refinement is a local experimental input.** It must not be presented as normative CAWG, C2PA, TRQP, or ToIP text.
3. **No universal agent credential format is selected.** ANAB/ARPA/TIS concepts are composed at the assurance layer without declaring a single mandatory wire representation.
4. **A2A is treated as interaction/discovery, not authority.** This case does not claim that A2A itself supplies delegated authority.
5. **Decision policy remains application-specific.** The lab tests separation of verification and decision authority but does not standardize sector-specific legal, safety, clinical, financial, or administrative policy.
6. **Historical resolution is bounded by source retention and reconstruction quality.** A requested-time answer can only be as strong as the authoritative lifecycle evidence available.
7. **Model and runtime identity are not assumed equivalent.** The case identifies the acting agent/runtime as evidence permits; it does not claim that model identity alone establishes accountable actor identity.
8. **Sub-agent/tool chains remain an experimental surface.** The current vectors cover scope attenuation and principal continuity but not every nested tool or multi-tenant execution topology.
9. **Privacy and selective disclosure are not fully profiled.** Evidence portability must not be interpreted as a requirement to disclose all principal, agent, or content metadata to every relying party.
10. **DCAS evaluation is not certification.** Repository-controlled evaluation evidence remains distinct from independent assessment.
