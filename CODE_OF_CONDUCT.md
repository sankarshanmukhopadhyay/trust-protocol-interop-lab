# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this repository should be professional, evidence-oriented, and respectful. Contributors are expected to critique claims, designs, evidence, and implementation choices without personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct.

Good-faith disagreement is welcome, including adversarial review of interoperability, authority, privacy, security, governance, and assurance claims. The purpose of review is to improve falsifiability and safety, not to suppress dissent.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- distinguish evidence from inference and proposal;
- identify relevant scope, assumptions, and authority boundaries;
- correct material errors transparently;
- avoid publishing private or security-sensitive information without authorization;
- respect upstream project governance and attribution; and
- keep issue, review, and discussion threads focused on the technical or governance question at hand.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, or knowingly deceptive representation of evidence or authority is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy and may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or participation that materially violates it. Enforcement actions should be proportionate and, where practical, leave an auditable repository record.

Security-sensitive reports are governed by [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
